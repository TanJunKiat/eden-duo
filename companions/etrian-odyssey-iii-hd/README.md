# Etrian Odyssey III HD experimental companion

Maintainers: see the [three-game implementation and research handoff](../../docs/etrian-companions-maintainer-guide.md).

For Eden Duo runtime 25, install `010084A0176AA000-v0.1.18.dsmod.zip` through **Add-ons → Install → Dual screen mods**. This Android ARM64 package requires EO III HD's updated main build ID `3D0322EDA242DEC85321974C126F43AC00000000000000000000000000000000`. It retains the Armoroad checklist and a read-only companion map. Edit the native map on the main game screen.

Version **0.1.4 — Abyss Glass** adds localized enemy names to the battle panel. The three panels are:

- **Armoroad:** active party cards in party order with current HP/TP and a distinct KO state. Empty and unreadable parties get different messages. Guild-only members and battle-only maxima are not shown.
- **Dungeon:** the fixed recorded-exploration map retains the full grid, drawn walls, icon fallbacks, and player heading.
- **Battle:** enemies appear on the stage above the party HUD. Cards follow the readable roster order, not verified in-game positions, and keep readable KO members visible. There are no Front/Back labels. The panel reports effects as unavailable; once verified, effects will use skill names, not stat abbreviations.

The companion does not write to the game. The v0.1.4 ZIP shows recorded exploration only. III's sea maps are not supported; unknown scenes show an unavailable screen rather than a stale dungeon map. Drawing and memo text are unsupported.

## Labyrinth map hints (v0.1.9 trial)

The v0.1.10 trial keeps these hints and corrects the player arrow and compass label, which were swapped for east and south.

The trial starts on the full recorded map with **Hints: Off**. Tap it on B1F–B25F to show geometry and dim reference icons together. **Spoilers:** this reveals stairs, treasure, gathering spots, hidden shortcuts, and possible event locations, along with other identifiable features such as doors and save points. Event markers do not say whether an event is currently available. Shortcut arrows identify a passage, not its direction of travel.

Hint icons appear only on unrecorded cells without a player marker. Recorded tiles, walls, markers, and player heading take priority. Unknown icons and unreliable locations are omitted. The loaded geometry must match the current floor's indexed game asset and dimensions; missing or mismatched geometry leaves the recorded map alone. Sea maps remain unsupported. The previous v0.1.8 trial showed geometry only.

**Floor Guide (v0.1.11 trial)** covers **B1F–B25F**. It lists the current floor's standard encounters using the installed game's active language and shows Next/Previous when there are more than 12 monsters. Tap a monster for localized item drops and ordinary base chances per defeated monster before modifiers. Conditional items are listed, but their requirements and chances are marked unverified. Special encounter variants and monster encounter odds aren't shown. **Back to List** returns to the roster; **Back to Map** preserves the previous hint setting. Town and battle dismiss the guide. Map geometry and monster data are read from the installed game, not bundled. The checked-in v0.1.4 ZIP is unchanged.

**Expedition strip (v0.1.11 trial).** The bottom of the full-size dungeon map shows carried Ariadne Threads, free bag slots, and counts of KO members and living members with 5 TP or less. Tap it to see exact current HP/TP in party order. A dash means unavailable, not zero. Back to Map preserves your hint setting.

**Armoroad departure checklist (v0.1.14).** Front and Back cards use the game's active formation slots. Each card shows current HP and TP, with a KO badge or a low-TP cue (5 TP or less for a living member). The panel shows free item slots, carried Ariadne Threads, and only quests and missions the game currently marks ready to report. Known turn-in locations sit beside their names; tap **View all** when more than two are ready. Missing data is marked unavailable without hiding other sections. Included in both the v0.1.14 runtime 26 trial and the v0.1.15 runtime 25 package.

**Armoroad Quest Board (v0.1.17).** Party cards show current/max HP and TP independently when the corresponding town maximum is readable and valid. The checklist keeps its two ready-to-report previews. Tap **Quest Board** to switch between **Available**, **Accepted**, and **Ready to report**. Available shows only optional quests posted for Armoroad that have not been taken; locked and completed entries stay hidden. Main missions appear only under Accepted or Ready to report. The board opens to Ready to report when it has entries, otherwise to Available. An unreadable list is marked unavailable rather than empty. The board does not accept or turn in quests, and the dungeon Quest Tracker is unchanged.

**Optional Battle EXP Boost trial (v0.1.20).** Install `010084A0176AA000-v0.1.20.dsmod.zip` instead of the regular read-only v0.1.18 companion, then fully close and relaunch the game so Eden can apply the build-checked patch at launch. In Armoroad, select **Off**, **2×**, **5×**, or **10×** in the top right. Off is normal EXP. The boost targets future battle rewards only, keeps the game's reward cap, and returns to Off on reload. Switching Off does not undo earned EXP. Start on a backup save. The regular ZIP remains available to reinstall.

**Optional Deep Run trial (v0.1.21).** In addition to EXP Boost, the trial adds a **Deep Run** switch in Armoroad. It starts Off. With it On, the map shows the next victory's reward factor: 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, then 2× for the fifth and later wins in that outing. Escapes do not advance the counter. A return to town or a reload ends the streak. Deep Run and the separate EXP Boost multiply together, subject to the game's existing EXP cap. Install the new ZIP and fully relaunch the game. Guild Training, rotation, the bounty, and the sale bonus are not part of this trial.

**Optional Expedition Rules trial (v0.1.22).** Use `010084A0176AA000-v0.1.22.dsmod.zip` for EO III HD v0.3.0 on Android ARM64 with Eden Duo runtime 25. In Armoroad, tap **Guild Training** to cycle Off → 50% → 100%, and tap **Guild Rotation** or **Merchant’s Favor** to toggle them. All rules start Off. Training gives reserves the larger of native Combat Study or the selected unboosted share; Rotation adds half that unboosted share for eligible living lower-level active recruits. Favor rolls after a paid stay and, when **Ready**, increases the next eligible material-only sale by 50%, rounded down, with a 5,000 en bonus cap. EXP Boost and Deep Run remain available. **Bistro Bounty is not included.** This is a trial: core award and sale paths were exercised in desktop research; the packaged trial has not been replayed on physical Android. Install through **Add-ons → Install → Dual screen mods**, then fully relaunch the game.

**Optional Bistro Bounty trial (v0.1.23).** Install `010084A0176AA000-v0.1.23.dsmod.zip` instead of v0.1.22, then fully relaunch. Visit a labyrinth floor in the current session, return to Armoroad, and tap **Accept bounty** at the bottom of Abyss Glass. The companion draws one regular monster species from the standard encounters of floors you've visited this session. Defeat five of that species in ordinary victories. When ready, tap **Prepare report**, then choose **Report results** at the Butterfly Bistro and confirm the native EXP screen. Each active member earns 10% of their current level's full EXP interval, rounded down, independently of battle boosts; capped members get none. Bounties are session-only and reset on reload. FOEs are not included. This is a trial; use a backup save. The desktop lab exercised acceptance, ordinary-victory filtering, and the native claim with a staged completed contract. Physical Android and an unstaged five-kill playthrough have not been verified.

**Optional Armoroad Rooms trial (v0.1.25).** Install `010084A0176AA000-v0.1.25.dsmod.zip` instead of the earlier trials. This fixes v0.1.24 mistaking a successful inn save for a reload and resetting the selected rules. Use **Add-ons → Install → Dual screen mods** and fully relaunch EO III HD v0.3.0 on Android ARM64 with Eden Duo runtime 25. The plaza is a departure checklist; open **Settings** there to opt into rules. The Bistro offers up to four regular bounties with one active contract, the Guild lists active members and reserves, and Via Senatus shows accepted and report-ready mission statuses without guessed counters.

**Weekly Buyers’ List** replaces Merchant’s Favor. Turn it on in Settings and visit the labyrinth. At Napier’s Firm, the companion lists up to four distinct materials from the deepest eligible floor visited this session: **+15%, +20%, +30%, −25%**. The list rotates every seven in-game days; descending farther changes the next list, not the current one. Prices round down per unit with a 1 en minimum on normally positive prices. The companion shows the normal and adjusted unit price once the native Sell menu has quoted that material. Equipment and unlisted items use normal prices. All rules start Off and reset on reload; visiting rooms and resting at the inn do not toggle them off. Use a backup save: native confirmation and payout were tested in the desktop lab with an isolated quote fixture; physical Android has not been tested.

**Native map trial (v0.1.13–v0.1.14 only).** Requires the updated Eden Duo **DSMod runtime 26**, handheld mode, and a physical companion touchscreen. Open EO III's map with **X** and its icon palette with **+**. The companion shows the native map editor; drag from the game's palette onto the map. Use the game's Undo and save normally. Close the editor to return to Abyss Glass. Simulated/inline second screens retain the read-only companion view. Native wall and tile drawing are not verified.

**Enemy cards (v0.1.16).** The panel uses the game's alive flags to remove defeated monsters even if their roster slots remain occupied. KO allies stay visible.

**Battle timer (v0.1.12; menu fix in v0.1.18).** During command selection, a living ally with verified **Guard Order** shows its localized skill name and remaining turns. The timer stays visible when opening the Skill list or choosing a target. It is hidden during action animations and disappears when the buff expires. No other buffs, debuffs, binds, or ailments have timers.

## Quest Tracker trial (v0.1.7)

From the dungeon map, tap **Quests**. Main missions come first, then active side quests you can work on from your current floor. Tap a row for the objective, locations and sources, required items, and turn-in point where known. Only active and report-ready entries appear; completed and unaccepted quests stay hidden. **Back to List** and **Back to Map** return without changing the map or hints.

The installed HD game supplies journal state, localized names and fallback objective text. The companion adds English guide hints—even where the journal is vague—based on the [EO III HD Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2994339871) and [Final Weapon's HD quest guide](https://finalweapon.net/2023/06/05/etrian-odyssey-3-quests-list/), matched to the installed game's entries. Incomplete or conflicting information falls back to journal text or omits the unverified detail. **In bag** is carried inventory, not quest credit. The tracker shows the game's active/report-ready flags; it does not estimate kill, mapping, event or gathering counters. It does not open in town, battle or at sea.

The B1F main mission and touch navigation were checked in an isolated macOS session. Side-quest flags and inventory transitions have synthetic coverage; late-game hints were guide-checked, not individually replayed. Physical Android testing is still outstanding.

Map artwork is read from the installed game's compressed Unity bundle at runtime. Validated icons fall back to built-in approximations if the bundle or texture metadata cannot be read. The icon-to-map-symbol mapping still needs visual validation. Enemy names come from the game's active text table (English, German, French, Italian, or Spanish). If the lookup is unavailable and the unit has no readable name of its own, the card falls back to `ENEMY <slot>`. Effect identities and durations remain unknown, and the first game's War Cry countdown is not reused.

The Armoroad reader was verified on the updated game in an isolated macOS developer session at the town hub, Aman’s Inn, and the Explorers Guild. A guild formation change updated the panel from one active member (44 HP / 18 TP) to two (50 HP / 24 TP first, 44 HP / 18 TP second), even though the two names were identical. A synthetic test covers refresh after HP/TP changes. An in-game before/after rest and installation on physical Android hardware have not been verified.

An isolated macOS smoke test of Abyss Glass showed the Armoroad roster, the B1F map during exploration, and a two-enemy encounter with one ally whose 44 HP / 18 TP matched the game. The v0.1.4 battle cards matched the in-game target names for both enemies in the English encounter. Battle maxima, enemy HP after damage, exact formation positions, and the full icon mapping need further live validation. Enemy names support Western European accents; other player-name characters still render as `?`.
